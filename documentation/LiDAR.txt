LIDAR setup

Download attached driver if on windows 11 (CP210x_VCP_Windows.zip).
Download robostudios from https://www.abb.com/global/en/areas/robotics/downloads 
After opening robostudios, click register and create an account.
In the robostudio app, enter your account information and login.
Once robostudio opens, click LiDAR -> right click on the side panel and click manual.
Click ok, then click the run button to start scanning.

Note: this software allows experimentation on a windows machine. This is not how the Raspberry Pi will communicate with the Lidar.
